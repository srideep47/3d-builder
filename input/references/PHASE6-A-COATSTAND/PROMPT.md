# Phase 6 subject A — wooden coat stand (clothes tree)

Order: a floor-standing wooden coat stand for a hallway, overall size
480 x 480 x 1750 mm, complexity: simple, under 30,000 triangles.

## Description

A classic turned-wood clothes tree: one central pole on a round metal base,
with two rings of straight pegs for coats and hats.

- **Base**: a round disc of matte black powder-coated steel, 480 mm across
  and 30 mm thick, resting flat on the floor.
- **Pole**: a turned walnut cylinder 56 mm in diameter, rising from the top
  of the base disc to 1690 mm above the floor.
- **Finial**: a turned walnut ball 120 mm in diameter centered on top of the
  pole (its center sits at the pole top, 1690 mm), crowned at the overall
  height.
- **Upper peg ring**: four walnut pegs at 1600 mm height, evenly spaced every
  90 degrees around the pole. Each peg is a straight cylinder 28 mm in
  diameter and 130 mm long, horizontal, pointing radially outward, its inner
  end embedded in the pole.
- **Lower peg ring**: four walnut pegs at 1450 mm height, evenly spaced every
  90 degrees, rotated 45 degrees relative to the upper ring. Each peg is a
  straight cylinder 24 mm in diameter and 110 mm long, horizontal, pointing
  radially outward, inner end embedded in the pole.

The lowest peg ring leaves hanging clearance for long coats; the whole stand
is symmetric about the central vertical axis. The base disc is the only part
touching the floor.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 480 x 480 x 1750 mm (width by depth by height).
- Base disc: 480 mm diameter, 30 mm thick.
- Pole: 56 mm diameter, top at 1690 mm.
- Finial ball: 120 mm diameter.
- Upper pegs: 28 mm diameter, 130 mm long, centers at 1600 mm height.
- Lower pegs: 24 mm diameter, 110 mm long, centers at 1450 mm height.

## Materials

- Pole, finial, and pegs: walnut wood, satin finish.
- Base disc: matte black powder-coated metal.
