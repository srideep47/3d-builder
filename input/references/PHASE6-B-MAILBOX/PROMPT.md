# Phase 6 subject B — steel mailbox

Order: a wall-mounted steel mailbox for a residential entry, overall size
260 x 130 x 200 mm, complexity: simple, under 20,000 triangles.

## Description

A classic rounded-top sheet-steel mailbox of the kind fixed beside a front
door, mounted with its closed back against the wall (no wall is modeled; the
mailbox itself is the whole object, resting with its bottom face at ground
level for the build).

- **Body**: a rectangular lower section 260 mm wide (spanning along the wall
  side to side), 130 mm front-to-back, and 135 mm tall, closed on all sides.
  Its top is a half-round dome — a semicircular arch spanning the 130 mm
  depth, running the full 260 mm width — so the body reaches its overall
  height of 200 mm at the ridge of the dome.
- **Door**: a flat steel panel set into the front face, 244 mm wide by
  118 mm tall and 3 mm thick, centered on the front, separated from the
  surrounding body by an even reveal gap on all four sides. The door covers
  the whole front opening below the start of the curve.
- **Signal flag**: a small notifier flag on the right side of the body near
  the top: a short pivot post 8 mm in diameter and 40 mm long standing off
  the side face, carrying a flat rectangular flag plate 60 mm by 40 mm and
  3 mm thick, raised diagonally at 45 degrees above horizontal.
- The bottom is closed and flat; the back is closed and flat.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 260 x 130 x 200 mm (width by depth by height).
- Lower body: 260 mm wide, 130 mm deep, 135 mm tall; dome adds 65 mm
  (the arch radius is half the 130 mm depth).
- Door panel: 244 mm wide by 118 mm tall by 3 mm thick.
- Flag post: 8 mm diameter by 40 mm long; flag plate 60 mm by 40 mm by
  3 mm thick.

## Materials

- Body and door: brushed galvanized steel.
- Signal flag (post and plate): matte black.
