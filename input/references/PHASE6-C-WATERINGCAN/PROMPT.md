# Phase 6 subject C — metal watering can

Order: a galvanized steel watering can for the garden, a free-standing
vessel that rests on the ground, overall size 380 x 200 x 340 mm,
complexity: simple, under 25,000 triangles.

## Description

A classic galvanized-metal watering can: a cylindrical body with a long
curved pouring spout on one side and an arched carrying handle over the top.

- **Body**: a straight cylinder 200 mm in diameter and 260 mm tall, with a
  plain rolled rim (a small bevel on the top edge is enough; no separate rim
  part). The flat base rests on the ground.
- **Spout**: a round tube 26 mm in outside diameter. It leaves the body wall
  about 140 mm above the base, sweeps outward and upward on the front side,
  and ends with its tip (including the rose) reaching 380 mm forward of the
  back of the can, at roughly 295 mm height. The spout's inner end is
  embedded in the body wall so the joint is watertight in appearance.
- **Rose**: a flat round sprinkler head 70 mm in diameter and 12 mm thick at
  the spout tip, tilted to face along the spout's final direction.
- **Handle**: an arch over the top made of the same 20 mm round tube,
  springing from attachments on the body rim about 75 mm either side of the
  centerline and peaking 330 mm above the ground at the arch crown, so the
  handle tube's top defines the overall height.

The can is symmetric about its vertical center plane (the plane containing
the spout direction); the spout points along the length axis, the handle
arch spans the width direction.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 380 x 200 x 340 mm (length by width by height, spout direction
  along the length).
- Body: 200 mm diameter, 260 mm tall.
- Spout tube: 26 mm outside diameter; leaves the body at about 140 mm
  height; tip reach 380 mm from the back of the can at about 295 mm height.
- Rose: 70 mm diameter, 12 mm thick.
- Handle tube: 20 mm outside diameter; attachments at the rim about 75 mm
  either side of the centerline; crown of the arch at 330 mm.

## Materials

- Body, spout, rose, and handle: brushed galvanized steel.
