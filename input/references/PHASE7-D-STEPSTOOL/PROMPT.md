# Phase 7 subject D — wooden two-step step stool

Order: a floor-standing wooden two-step step stool for a kitchen, overall
size 450 x 420 x 480 mm, complexity: medium, polycount ceiling 25,000.

## Description

A classic wooden kitchen step stool: two vertical side panels carry two
stacked treads, the lower one at the front and the top step directly behind
it. The length runs front-to-back (along the climbing direction) and the
width side-to-side.

- **Side panels**: two beech panels, each 20 mm thick, 450 mm long and
  480 mm tall, standing on edge parallel to each other with their outer
  faces 420 mm apart. Their top edges are level with the upper tread.
- **Lower tread**: a beech board 420 mm wide, 225 mm deep and 25 mm thick,
  spanning the side panels at the front, its top surface 240 mm above the
  floor.
- **Upper tread**: a beech board 420 mm wide, 225 mm deep and 25 mm thick,
  spanning the side panels directly behind the lower tread, its top surface
  480 mm above the floor (the overall height).
- The side panels are the only parts touching the floor.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 450 x 420 x 480 mm (length front-to-back, width side-to-side,
  height floor-to-top).
- Side panels: 20 mm thick, 450 mm long, 480 mm tall, outer faces 420 mm apart.
- Lower tread: 420 x 225 mm footprint, 25 mm thick, top at 240 mm.
- Upper tread: 420 x 225 mm footprint, 25 mm thick, top at 480 mm.

## Materials

- Side panels and treads: beech wood with a clear satin varnish.
