# Phase 7 subject E — aluminium milk churn

Order: a floor-standing aluminium dairy milk churn, overall size
340 x 340 x 640 mm, complexity: simple, polycount ceiling 20,000.

## Description

A traditional tapered dairy milk churn: a slightly flared cylindrical body
rising from a narrow base to a wide shoulder, closed by a domed lid with a
central lift knob. The whole churn is symmetric about its vertical axis.

- **Body**: a tapered cylinder 560 mm tall from the floor, flaring from a
  300 mm diameter base to a 340 mm diameter shoulder at the top of the body.
- **Lid**: a shallow dome closing the full 340 mm shoulder, topped by a
  mushroom-shaped lift knob whose crown sits 640 mm above the floor (the
  overall height).
- The flat base is the only part touching the floor.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 340 x 340 x 640 mm (length and width across the shoulder,
  height floor-to-knob-crown).
- Body: 560 mm tall, 300 mm base diameter, 340 mm shoulder diameter.
- Lid knob: crown at 640 mm above the floor.

## Materials

- Body and lid: brushed aluminium.
- Knob: black phenolic plastic.
