# Phase 7 subject F — hand garden trowel

Order: a hand garden trowel modeled lying flat after use, so the blade
rests on the floor, overall size 320 x 70 x 45 mm, complexity: simple,
polycount ceiling 15,000.

## Description

A transplanting hand trowel lying flat on the ground, seen as it was set
down between uses: a pointed scoop blade, a short raised neck, and a
cylindrical grip in line behind it. The length runs from the blade tip to
the grip end; the width is the blade at its widest.

- **Blade**: a pointed scoop 160 mm long from tip to heel, 70 mm at its
  widest near the heel, and 3 mm thick, lying flat so the blade rests on
  the floor.
- **Neck**: a tapered shank 20 mm long joining the blade heel to the grip,
  lifting the grip axis clear of the floor.
- **Grip**: a stained-ash cylinder 32 mm in diameter and 140 mm long,
  horizontal and in line with the blade, its axis 29 mm above the floor so
  the grip crown reaches the overall height.
- The blade underside is the only part touching the floor.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 320 x 70 x 45 mm (length tip-to-grip-end, width across the
  blade, height floor-to-grip-crown).
- Blade: 160 mm long, 70 mm wide, 3 mm thick.
- Neck: 20 mm long.
- Grip: 32 mm diameter, 140 mm long, axis 29 mm above the floor.

## Materials

- Blade and neck: polished stainless steel.
- Grip: stained ash wood.
