# Phase 7 subject G — brass chamberstick

Order: a floor-standing brass chamberstick candle holder, overall size
190 x 140 x 65 mm, complexity: simple, polycount ceiling 20,000.

## Description

A traditional bedside chamberstick: a shallow circular drip pan with a
raised rolled rim, a central candle socket, and a flat loop handle reaching
off one side. The length runs from the handle's outer tip across the pan
center to the far rim; the width is the pan diameter.

- **Drip pan**: a shallow circular dish 140 mm in diameter with a raised
  rolled rim, 12 mm thick overall; the pan underside rests on the floor.
- **Socket**: a cylindrical candle socket of 25 mm outer diameter with
  3 mm walls, rising from the pan center; its top edge is 65 mm above the
  floor (the overall height).
- **Handle**: a flat loop 40 mm across, level with the pan rim, reaching
  50 mm beyond the rim on one side (to the overall length).
- The pan underside is the only part touching the floor.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 190 x 140 x 65 mm (length handle-tip-to-far-rim, width across
  the pan, height floor-to-socket-top).
- Drip pan: 140 mm diameter, 12 mm thick.
- Socket: 25 mm outer diameter, 3 mm walls, top edge at 65 mm.
- Handle: 40 mm across, reaching 50 mm beyond the rim.

## Materials

- All parts: polished brass.
