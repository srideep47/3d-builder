# Phase 7 subject H — galvanized steel water bucket

Order: a floor-standing galvanized steel water bucket, overall size
260 x 260 x 330 mm, complexity: simple, polycount ceiling 20,000.

## Description

A plain galvanized bucket: a tapered cylindrical body flaring from a flat
base to a rolled rim, with a semicircular bail handle pinned to lugs on
opposite sides of the rim. The bucket is symmetric about its vertical axis.

- **Body**: a tapered cylinder 250 mm tall from the floor, flaring from a
  210 mm diameter flat base to a 260 mm diameter rim.
- **Rim**: a rolled lip circling the top edge of the body at the 260 mm
  diameter.
- **Bail**: a semicircular arc handle pinned to two lugs on opposite sides
  of the rim, standing proud so its apex is 330 mm above the floor (the
  overall height).
- The flat base is the only part touching the floor.

## Stated dimensions (test constraints, not photo measurements)

- Overall: 260 x 260 x 330 mm (length and width across the rim, height
  floor-to-bail-apex).
- Body: 250 mm tall, 210 mm base diameter, 260 mm rim diameter.
- Bail: apex 330 mm above the floor.

## Materials

- Body, rim, lugs, and bail: galvanized steel.
